Wireless sensor networks consist of resource-constrained devices that sense their environment, process measurements, and communicate through wireless links. Designing these networks requires balancing sensing quality, communication reliability, latency, energy consumption, security, cost, and network lifetime. This course covers sensor-node hardware, wireless communication, network organization, routing, energy management, in-network processing, localization, security, data management, and representative applications. Students evaluate design trade-offs and, where materials are available, implement or simulate selected techniques.

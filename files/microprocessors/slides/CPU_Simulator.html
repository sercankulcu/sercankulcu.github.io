Numbers: decimal 42, hex 0x2A or binary 0b101010, all 0–255. A jump target t is a label (loop:) or an instruction number. Every instruction runs in five micro-steps: fetch, decode, operand, execute, write back. Flags change only at write back, so STA, OUT and jumps keep the flags of the previous ALU instruction.

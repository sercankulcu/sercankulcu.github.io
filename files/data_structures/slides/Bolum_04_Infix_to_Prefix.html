Infix to Prefix Converter

In infix notation, operators appear between operands, such as A + B. In prefix notation, the operator appears before its operands, such as + A B. Prefix expressions encode evaluation order directly and therefore do not require parentheses during evaluation.

Core idea

scan tokens from right to left

  append to temporary output

operator:
  pop higher-precedence operators
  push current operator

  reverse their stack behavior

finally:
  pop remaining operators
  reverse temporary output
  → prefix expression

Key ideas

Precedence

Multiplication and division bind more strongly than addition and subtraction; exponentiation has the highest supported precedence.

Associativity

Exponentiation is right-associative. The stack rule must treat equal-precedence exponent operators differently.

Why a stack?

Operators must wait until enough of the expression has been scanned to determine when they belong in the output.

Linear complexity

Each token is processed once, while each operator is pushed and popped at most once.

Infix to Postfix Converter

In infix notation, operators appear between operands, such as A + B. In postfix notation, operators appear after their operands, such as A B +. Postfix notation removes the need for parentheses during evaluation because precedence has already been encoded in the token order.

"(":

")":
  pop until "("

finally:
  pop remaining operators

Key ideas

Precedence

Higher-precedence operators must reach the output before lower-precedence operators that follow them.

Associativity

Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division are left-associative; exponentiation is right-associative.

Parentheses

Parentheses temporarily control the operator stack but never appear in the final postfix expression.

Linear complexity

Each token is processed once, while each operator is pushed and popped at most once.

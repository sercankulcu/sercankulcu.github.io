Tower of Hanoi Simulator

The Tower of Hanoi is a recursive puzzle with three towers and disks of different sizes. Move the complete stack from Tower A to Tower C while moving only one disk at a time, moving only the top disk, and never placing a larger disk on a smaller disk.

Minimum moves7
RecurrenceT(n) = 2T(n − 1) + 1
Time complexityO(2ⁿ)
Recursion depthO(n)

    Recursive structure

    hanoi(n, source, auxiliary, target):
  if n == 1:
    move source → target
  else:
    hanoi(n-1, source, target, auxiliary)
    move source → target
    hanoi(n-1, auxiliary, source, target)

    Why this puzzle matters

    Recursive decomposition

    A problem with n disks is reduced to two smaller problems with n − 1 disks plus one direct move.

    Exponential growth

    The optimal number of moves is 2ⁿ − 1, so adding one disk nearly doubles the required work.

    Stack connection

    Recursive calls are managed by the call stack. Each unfinished call waits while a smaller subproblem is solved.

    Invariant

    At every valid state, disks on each tower remain ordered from largest at the bottom to smallest at the top.

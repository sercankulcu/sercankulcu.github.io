How this teaches game theory

Public-goods games model the tension between private incentives and collective welfare. Contributing benefits everyone, but each player may prefer to keep their own endowment and free-ride on others.

Complete scenario, mode, level, and concept guide

Scenarios

Basic public good

All contributions are multiplied and shared equally. Individual incentives can conflict with the group's best outcome.

Threshold project

The group receives a large public benefit only if total contributions reach a target.

Contribution with punishment

Very low contributors lose a small penalty, illustrating how institutions can change incentives.

Strategies

Always Cooperate / Always Defect

Useful baselines that expose exploitation and the short-run temptation to defect.

Tit-for-Tat

Starts cooperative, then copies the opponent's previous move. It combines reciprocity and immediate forgiveness.

Grim Trigger

Cooperates until the opponent defects once, then defects forever.

Generous Tit-for-Tat

Usually reciprocates defection but occasionally forgives, making it more robust to noise.

Win-Stay, Lose-Shift

Repeats a move after a good outcome and switches after a poor one.

Levels

Level 1 — 20 Rounds

Short horizon for quickly understanding strategy logic.

Level 2 — 100 Rounds

Longer interaction makes reputation and retaliation more consequential.

Level 3 — Noise

One hundred rounds with accidental move flips, testing forgiveness and robustness.

What to observe: no strategy is universally best. Performance depends on the population of opponents, the horizon, and whether mistakes occur.

How to play

  1. You play X and the AI plays O. Click a square (or select it with Tab and press Enter) to place your mark.
  2. The first player to fill three squares in a row horizontally, vertically or diagonally wins; if the board fills up, the game is a draw.
  3. Use the First move option to choose who starts the game.

Minimax search

For every empty square, the AI tries all continuations to the end of the game. Endings it wins score +10, endings it loses score −10, and a draw scores 0; the depth is subtracted from the score so that faster wins and slower losses are preferred. On its own turn it picks the highest value, on the opponent's turn the lowest.

Alpha-beta pruning

As soon as it becomes clear that a branch cannot do better than an option already found, the rest of that branch is skipped. The result is the same; only fewer nodes are searched. The counter on the right shows how many nodes were examined for each move.

Colors on the board

  • Orange square: the search is currently trying this move.
  • Green square and the number inside: the minimax value of this move (from the AI's point of view).
  • If Animate search is turned off, the AI moves immediately.

Game theory connection

Tic Tac Toe is a small, zero-sum game of perfect information. If both sides play optimally the result is a draw: the minimax opponent cannot be beaten; at best you can hold it to a draw.

English auction (open ascending bid)

Six bidders have private valuations. The auction starts at $0. In each round the auctioneer raises the price (by $5 in rounds 1–5, $10 in rounds 6–15, then $15) and every bidder whose valuation is below the new price drops out.

Rule: the last remaining bidder wins and pays the final price. If all remaining bidders would drop out in the same round, the one with the highest valuation wins at the previous price.

How to use

  • Start auction opens the clock; Next round raises the price one step.
  • Green cards are still in, red cards have dropped out.
  • New bidders draws fresh valuations.

What this shows

The dominant strategy is to stay in until the price exceeds your valuation. The bidder with the highest valuation therefore wins and pays (up to one price step) the second-highest valuation, exactly like a Vickrey (second-price sealed-bid) auction. The result panel compares the payment with the second-highest valuation.

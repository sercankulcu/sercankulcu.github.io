Vickrey auction (second-price sealed bid)

Each bidder writes a secret bid in a sealed envelope. The highest bidder wins the item but pays the second-highest bid. Ties go to the bidder listed first (and then the price equals the tied bid).

How to use

  • Every bidder has a private valuation (shown in the middle column). Type a bid for each bidder; Enter opens the envelopes.
  • Bid = valuation fills in truthful bids; New valuations draws new values and clears the bids.
  • The payoff of the winner is valuation − price; losers get 0. The bidder who sets the price is marked.

What this shows

Truthful bidding (bid = valuation) is a weakly dominant strategy: your bid only decides whether you win, never what you pay. Overbidding can make you win at a price above your valuation (negative payoff); underbidding can make you lose an item you would have bought at a profit.

Try it: start from truthful bids, change one bidder's bid up or down and check that their payoff never improves. The English auction reaches the same outcome with an open, ascending clock.

First-price sealed-bid auction

Each bidder writes a secret bid in a sealed envelope. When the envelopes are opened, the highest bidder wins the item and pays exactly their own bid. Ties go to the bidder listed first.

How to use

  • Every bidder has a private valuation (shown in the middle column). Type a bid for each bidder; Enter opens the envelopes.
  • Bid = valuation fills in truthful bids; New valuations draws new values and clears the bids.
  • The payoff of the winner is valuation − bid; losers get 0.

What this shows

Truthful bidding is not dominant here: a winner who bids their valuation gets payoff 0. Bidders should shade their bids below their valuations. With n bidders and valuations drawn uniformly, the symmetric equilibrium bid is (n−1)/n of the valuation.

Try it: bid truthfully, then shade one bidder's bid and compare the payoffs. By revenue equivalence, the expected revenue matches the second-price (Vickrey) and Dutch auctions.

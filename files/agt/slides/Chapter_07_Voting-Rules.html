Voting Rules and Manipulation

← Chapter 7

Ballots

Cast your sincere vote, then (Level 3) try a strategic one.

How to play

There are three candidates: A, B and C. Every ballot is a full ranking. Pick a ranking with the six buttons; the scores, the winner and the ballot table update at once. Different aggregation rules can pick different winners from the same preferences, and a voter can sometimes gain by misreporting preferences (Gibbard–Satterthwaite: every non-dictatorial rule with three or more candidates can be manipulated at some profile).

Ties are broken alphabetically (A before B, B before C); the winner box notes this. In the table, V1, V2, … are the voters.

Voting rules

  • Plurality: only the first-ranked candidate scores. Watch for vote splitting, spoiler candidates and the incentive to abandon a sincere favorite for a luckier one.
  • Borda count: 2 points for first, 1 for second, 0 for last. A broad compromise candidate can beat a polarizing one; changing lower ranks can change the winner.
  • Approval (top two): the top two candidates on each ballot are approved and get 1 point each. Approving a compromise candidate, or withholding approval from a close rival, can be strategic.

Modes

  • You + bots: the computer generates all other ballots and you cast the last one. Clicking again replaces your ballot.
  • Build the electorate: you build the whole electorate. Each click adds the next ballot; once all seats are filled, a click replaces the latest ballot. Good for building ties, close races and paradoxes.
  • Simulation: every ballot, including the last, is generated. Press New election repeatedly to compare random profiles; the strategy test is run for the last voter.

Levels

  • Level 1 — 5 voters: four generated ballots and yours; scores are easy to check by hand.
  • Level 2 — 9 voters: more varied profiles; the visible effect of a single ballot shrinks.
  • Level 3 — Strategic voter: nine voters; Try strategic vote tests every alternative ranking for the last ballot. It reports a ballot that elects a candidate the voter sincerely ranks higher, or tells you that sincere voting is already best here.

Suggested path

  1. Plurality, You + bots, Level 1: learn the score counting.
  2. Keep the electorate and switch to Borda count and Approval: the rule changes, the ballots do not.
  3. Level 2: how much does a single ballot still matter?
  4. Level 3: cast a sincere ballot, note the winner and press Try strategic vote.

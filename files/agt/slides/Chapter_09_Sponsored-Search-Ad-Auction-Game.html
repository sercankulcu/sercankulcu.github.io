    How to play

    Compete for three ranked advertising slots using bids, quality scores, click-through rates (CTR) and auction pricing rules. Set your bid per click with the slider and press Run auction. Find best response searches every bid from 1 to 12 and picks the one that maximises your expected utility against the current competitors. New market draws new values, qualities and bot bids.

    Ads are ranked by rank score = bid × quality. Your utility is (value per click − price per click) × expected clicks; with no slot your utility is 0.

    Why this is game theory

    Sponsored-search auctions are multi-slot allocation mechanisms. Advertisers care about position, expected clicks, conversion value and price. The platform must rank advertisers while creating incentives that balance revenue and efficient allocation.

    Scenarios

    • Search Ads — steep CTR curve (12%, 7%, 3.5%): higher positions receive many more clicks. Quality and bid jointly determine ranking.
    • Social Media Ads — engagement quality varies a lot (0.3–1.0), so a lower bid with high quality can outrank a weak high bidder.
    • Sponsored Products — flatter CTR curve (10%, 6%, 4%): lower slots are still valuable, relevance influences ranking.

    Modes

    • Human Advertiser vs Bots — you choose one bid while competing against fixed bot advertisers.
    • Human + Bid Advisor — after every auction the advisor reports whether another bid would increase your expected utility.
    • Human Controls Market — your slider also shifts competing bids (each bot bids 0.8 less than the previous one), making strategic interactions easier to inspect.
    • Computer Simulation — all bidders, including you, shade their bids automatically to 65–95% of their value.

    Levels

    • Level 1 — First-Price Ranking: winners pay their own bid per click. Bid shading becomes immediately important.
    • Level 2 — GSP Pricing: ranking uses bid × quality, while payment per click is the next competitor's rank score divided by your quality (never more than your bid).
    • Level 3 — Strategic Bidding: GSP pricing, and before every auction each bot moves its bid to its own best response against the current bids.

    Key idea

    The highest bid does not always win the best slot when quality matters. Payment rules change bidding incentives: under GSP truthful bidding is not always a best response, but bids far above value are no longer as costly as in a first-price auction.

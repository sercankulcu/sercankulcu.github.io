Algorithm Guide

First Fit: choose the first hole large enough.

Best Fit: choose the smallest hole that can satisfy the request.

Worst Fit: choose the largest available hole.

Next Fit: like First Fit, but the search starts at the address right after the previous allocation (including the leftover part of the hole that was just split) and wraps around to the beginning of memory. The cursor is stored as an address, so freeing and coalescing do not move it; if the cursor lies inside a coalesced hole, that whole hole is examined first.

Hatched SYS blocks are reserved by the operating system and cannot be freed; user process IDs may not start with “SYS”. Block widths are proportional to their size (very small blocks keep a minimum width so their labels stay readable).

External fragmentation is approximated here as total free memory minus the largest free block. A high value means free space is split across many smaller holes.

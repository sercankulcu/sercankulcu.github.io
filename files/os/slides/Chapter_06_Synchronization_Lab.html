Concept Guide

How the simulator runs: each step, the scheduler picks the next actor in round-robin order that is not blocked and executes exactly one operation of its program. wait(S) decrements S if it is positive; otherwise the actor is blocked and appended to S's FIFO queue, and it stays blocked until someone signals S. signal(S) wakes the first waiter, which immediately owns the permit (S stays unchanged); with no waiter it increments S. If every actor is blocked, a deadlock is reported.

Mutex: only one actor may enter the critical section at a time. Switch to “Unsynchronized” to see a lost update: two actors read the same old counter value, and one increment overwrites the other.

Semaphore: allows a limited number of actors to access a shared resource concurrently.

Producer–Consumer: producers add items to a bounded buffer while consumers remove them. empty counts free slots, full counts filled slots, and mutex protects the buffer, preventing overflow and underflow.

Readers–Writers: multiple readers can access data together, but writers require exclusive access.

Dining Philosophers: each philosopher needs its two neighbouring forks. Taking the left fork first lets all philosophers hold one fork and wait forever (circular wait). Acquiring the lower-numbered fork first imposes a global order on resources and prevents the deadlock.

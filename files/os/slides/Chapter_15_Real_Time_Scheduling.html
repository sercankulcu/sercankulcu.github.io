Help
RM
Rate Monotonic: fixed priority, shorter period T first.
DM
Deadline Monotonic: fixed priority by D; used automatically when some D < T.
EDF
Earliest Deadline First: the nearest absolute deadline runs.
U
Utilization C/T; ΣU > 1 means no scheduler can keep up.
RM bound
ΣU ≤ n(21/n − 1) guarantees RM (sufficient, not necessary; D = T only).
EDF test
D = T: schedulable iff ΣU ≤ 1. D < T: density ΣC/D ≤ 1 is sufficient.
LCM
Hyperperiod: the schedule repeats after the LCM of the periods.
Rules
One CPU, integer time, all tasks released at 0, a job still unfinished at its deadline is aborted, ties go to the lower task number.

Introduction

What Question Does It Ask?

How is a realistic image produced from the description of a scene?

Computer graphics studies how to render an image from a scene described by geometry, materials, lights and a camera. It is the inverse of computer vision: vision recovers the scene from an image, graphics produces the image from the scene.

Introduction

A Short History

1963

Sutherland's Sketchpad shows drawing on screen with a light pen; interactive graphics begins.

1975

Phong publishes his reflection model and shading method; shiny surfaces look real.

1980

Whitted's ray tracing unifies reflection, refraction and shadows in one method.

1986

Kajiya publishes the rendering equation: the basis of physically based rendering.

1995

Toy Story becomes the first fully computer-generated feature film.

2018

Hardware ray tracing reaches consumer GPUs; real-time ray tracing begins.

The Course

Core Concepts

Homogeneous coordinates

Writing translation, rotation and scaling as 4×4 matrices.

Model-view-projection

The chain of transforms from object space to screen space.

Triangle mesh

Representing surfaces with vertices and triangles.

Rasterization

Finding the pixels a triangle covers.

Z-buffer

Visibility by keeping the nearest surface per pixel.

Lighting model

Ambient, diffuse and specular: Phong, Blinn-Phong.

Texture mapping

Wrapping an image or pattern on a surface; UV coordinates.

Ray tracing

Shooting rays from the camera and tracing light paths backward.

The Course

A Typical Workflow

Example: In a game, a character's triangle mesh is projected to the screen by the world and camera matrices, rasterization finds the pixels each triangle covers, and a shader computes each pixel's color from light and texture. This repeats 60 times a second.

Try It

Shading a Sphere with Light

Every frame, a game engine computes how bright each pixel is from the surface normal and the light direction. Move the light with the sliders, switch the shading method, and click the sphere to see how one pixel is computed.

A graphics course teaches this computation: Lambert's law (I ∝ N·L), the Phong highlight and where to compute the normal. The GPU runs the same formula for millions of pixels every frame, in parallel.

Practice

Common Misconceptions

Common beliefIn fact
Computer graphics is just making games.Film, architecture, medicine, simulation and scientific visualization are graphics too.
Ray tracing is a new invention.The method dates from 1980; what is new is hardware that runs it in real time.
Graphics and computer vision are the same.Each is the inverse of the other; they share the camera model but go opposite ways.
More triangles are enough for realism.Light, shadow and material often matter more than geometric detail.

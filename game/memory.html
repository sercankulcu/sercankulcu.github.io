Memory

Moves: 0 Time: 0s Pairs: 0/8 Best: —

How to play

Match all eight pairs in as few moves as possible.

How to play

  • Tap a card to flip it. The timer starts on your first flip.
  • Open two cards at a time and try to find matching symbols.
  • Matched pairs stay visible. The game tracks your moves and elapsed time.
  • Your best time is kept in this browser.
  • Each new game randomly selects one of four emoji themes.

Controls

  • Mouse or touch: tap a card.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a card, then Enter or Space.
  • TR / EN switches the interface language.

The timer pauses while this window is open or the tab is hidden.

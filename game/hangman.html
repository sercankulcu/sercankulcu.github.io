Hangman

Guess the hidden word before six incorrect letters.

Score: 0 Lives: 6 Streak: 0

How to Play

  • Select a letter on screen or type it on your keyboard.
  • Correct letters are revealed in the hidden word.
  • Each incorrect letter costs one life.
  • The round ends after six incorrect guesses.
  • Winning increases both your score and streak.

Tic Tac Toe

Complete a row of three before the computer does.

You: 0 Computer: 0 Draws: 0

How to Play

  • You play X and always move first.
  • Easy chooses a random available square.
  • Normal looks for winning moves, otherwise plays randomly.
  • Hard uses minimax and cannot be beaten with perfect play.
  • Changing the difficulty starts a new round without resetting the scoreboard.

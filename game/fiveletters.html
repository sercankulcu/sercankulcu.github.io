Five Letters

Guess the hidden five-letter Turkish word in six attempts.

Attempt: 1/6 Streak: 0

How to Play

  • Guess the five-letter Turkish word in six attempts.
  • Green: correct letter in the correct position.
  • Orange: the letter is in the word but in another position.
  • Gray: the letter is not present in that remaining position.
  • You can use either the on-screen keyboard or a physical keyboard.

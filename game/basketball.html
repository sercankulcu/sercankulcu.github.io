How to Play

🏀 Shooting

Drag the ball and release it toward the hoop. Direction and release speed determine the shot.

🎯 Scoring

Each basket starts at 2 points. Consecutive baskets build a streak and add bonus points.

💡 Tip

Smooth upward swipes are usually more accurate than extremely powerful throws.

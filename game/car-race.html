    How to play

    Dodge traffic, survive the road, and chase your best score. The race gets faster and busier the longer you survive.

    How to play

    1. Press Start Race.
    2. Change lanes to avoid the oncoming cars.
    3. Pass close to traffic for Near Miss bonuses; near misses in a row build a combo.
    4. One crash ends the race. Your best scores are kept on the High Scores list.

    Controls

    • Desktop: ← → or A / D to change lanes.
    • Mobile: tap ◀ ▶ or tap a road lane.
    • Press P (or Ⅱ) to pause; 🔊 toggles sound.

    Notes

    • The race pauses when you switch tabs or open this help.

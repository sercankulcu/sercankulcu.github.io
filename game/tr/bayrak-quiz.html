How to play

Identify 10 country flags. Pick the country each flag belongs to.

Difficulty

  • Easy, Medium and Hard split the 197 countries the same way as the Capital City Quiz; Mixed draws from all of them.
  • Wrong answers come from the same difficulty as the flag, and flags that look almost the same (Chad and Romania, Indonesia and Monaco, Ireland and Côte d'Ivoire, Norway and Iceland…) are never offered together.
  • Changing the difficulty starts a new quiz.

Rules

  • Each quiz contains 10 different countries.
  • Each question has four answer choices.
  • A correct answer gives 10 points.
  • An incorrect answer subtracts 3 points, with a minimum score of zero.

Controls

  • Tap an answer, or press 1–4 on your keyboard.
  • New Quiz starts over with 10 new countries.

Records

Best is your highest score at the selected difficulty. Your best scores, the last difficulty you chose and the number of quizzes played are stored only on this device.

Language

The TR / EN button switches the interface and the country names between Turkish and English.

How to Play

🐇 Good Targets

Rabbit and turtle give points. Faster reactions earn more points, up to +10.

🐦‍⬛ Bad Targets

Crow and hedgehog are traps. Tapping one costs 5 points.

🔥 Difficulty

Easy uses longer target times. Hard adds more animal types and faster disappear times.

How to Play

🐇 Good Targets

Rabbit and turtle give points. Faster reactions earn more points: up to +10 for very quick taps.

🐦‍⬛ Bad Targets

Crow and hedgehog are traps. Tapping one costs 5 points, so react quickly but identify the animal first.

🔥 Difficulty

Easy mode uses rabbit and crow with longer target times. Hard mode adds turtle and hedgehog and makes targets disappear faster.

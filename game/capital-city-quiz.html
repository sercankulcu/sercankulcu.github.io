How to play

A multiple-choice quiz on countries and capital cities: countries & territories.

How well do you know the world's capitals?

  1. Choose a mode, question count, and difficulty to begin.
  2. Press Start Quiz and pick one of four answers.
  3. Go on with Next Question; Change settings takes you back to the setup.

Questions are randomized and do not repeat within a round.

Scoring

A correct answer gives 10 points, plus a streak bonus of 2 points per answer in a row (up to +10).

Keyboard

Answer with keys 1–4, then press Enter for the next question.

Notes

The quiz includes countries, microstates, and several commonly omitted entries.

Record is your best score for the current mode, question count and difficulty. Records and the games-played count are stored only on this device.

The TR / EN button switches the interface and the country and capital names between Turkish and English.

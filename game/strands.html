How to Play

  • Find five Turkish words connected to the displayed theme.
  • Letters in a word must come from neighboring tiles, including diagonals.
  • A tile cannot be used twice in the same word.
  • Tiles belonging to solved words remain unavailable.
  • Hint reveals the first letter and length of one unsolved word.
  • You may also type letters, use Backspace, and press Enter to check.

How to play

A quiz in the style of Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?: 10 questions · 10 prize steps · 3 lifelines. Prizes are in Turkish lira (₺).

The game

Each game picks 10 questions from the question bank. They go from easy to hard; every prize step brings a random question of its own difficulty level. Some questions are about Türkiye.

Lifelines

  • 50:50: Removes two wrong options.
  • Phone a Friend: Gives a suggestion that is likely to be right.
  • Ask the Audience: Shows percentages for the options.

Each lifeline can be used once per game. A new game resets the lifelines and picks new questions.

Keyboard

You can also answer with the A–D or 1–4 keys.

Record

Your record is the highest prize you have won. The record and the number of games played are stored only on this device.

Math Master

Solve as many arithmetic problems as possible in two minutes.

Score: 0 Streak: 0 Time: 02:00

How to Play

  • Enter the result using the number pad or physical keyboard.
  • ⌫ removes the last digit and C clears the answer.
  • Correct answers increase your streak and score bonus.
  • Wrong answers cost 2 points and reset the streak.
  • Skipping costs 1 point and reveals the answer.
  • The game lasts two minutes.

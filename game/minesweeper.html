How to Play

Reveal every safe square without opening a mine. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.

  • Desktop: left click reveals; right click flags.
  • Mobile: use Reveal / Flag mode, or long-press a covered square to flag it.
  • The first click and its immediate neighbors are always safe.
  • Tap an open number when it has the matching number of neighboring flags to chord-reveal the remaining neighbors.
  • You win when every non-mine square has been revealed.

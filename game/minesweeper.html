How to Play

Reveal every safe square without opening a mine. Numbers show how many mines are touching that square.

  • Desktop: left click reveals, right click places or removes a flag.
  • Mobile: use Reveal / Flag mode, or long-press a covered square to flag it.
  • First click is safe: the clicked square and its immediate neighbors never contain mines.
  • Chord reveal: tap an already-open number when the same number of neighboring flags is present to reveal its remaining neighbors.
  • You win when every non-mine square has been revealed.

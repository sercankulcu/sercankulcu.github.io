Red Light, Green Light

Reach the finish line without moving on red.

RED LIGHT
↑ forward · ← → sideways · hold the game area on mobile

Ready?

You control player 3.

Move only during the green light.

How to Play

  • You control player 3.
  • Move forward only while the light is green.
  • Attempting to move while the light is red ends the round.
  • Use ↑ to move forward and ← → to move sideways.
  • On touch devices, hold the game area or the ↑ button to move forward.
  • The other four players move automatically during green light.

How to Play

🏀 Shooting

Drag the basketball and release it toward the hoop. The direction and speed of your movement determine the shot.

🎯 Scoring

Every successful basket is worth 2 points. Consecutive baskets build a streak and can earn bonus points.

💡 Tip

Smooth upward swipes are usually more accurate than extremely powerful throws. Find the right rhythm and angle.

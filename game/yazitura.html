How to Play & Probability

Choose Heads or Tails, select a virtual bet, and flip the coin. A correct guess adds the bet to your balance; a wrong guess subtracts it. Each flip is independent and has a theoretical 50% probability for either side. Previous results and win streaks do not change the probability of the next flip.

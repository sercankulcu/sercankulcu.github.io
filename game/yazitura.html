    How to play

    Guess a side, flip the coin, and watch how chance behaves over many flips.

    Guess and flip

    Choose Heads (the portrait side) or Tails (the side with the number) and press Flip the Coin. The page counts your correct guesses and your accuracy. Whatever you pick, accuracy drifts toward 50% over time: no side and no pattern of guesses beats a fair coin.

    Runs

    A run is the same side coming up several times in a row. The current run and the longest heads and tails runs are kept. Long runs are normal: in 100 flips a run of six or seven is common.

    Simulation

    The 10 / 100 / 1000 / 10000 buttons flip a virtual coin that many times at once and show the tails share. Small samples swing widely; large ones settle close to 50% (the law of large numbers). Simulated flips are not added to your statistics.

    Independence

    Each flip is independent. After five heads in a row the next flip is still heads with probability 50%; tails is not "due". Results come from the browser's cryptographic random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues).

    Controls

    • Keyboard: H heads, T tails, Space or Enter to flip.
    • TR / EN switches the interface language.
    • Statistics stay in this browser until you press Reset Statistics (it asks for a second tap).

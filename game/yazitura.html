How to Play & Probability
Choose Heads or Tails, select a bet, and press Flip Coin. A correct guess adds your bet to the balance; a wrong guess subtracts it. You start with 100 coins.

Each flip is independent and has a theoretical 50% chance of landing on either side. Short sessions can look uneven, but over many flips the observed percentages often move closer to 50/50. The statistics panel lets you watch that happen.

Win streaks are tracked for fun, but they do not change the odds or multiply the payout. A previous result does not make either side more likely on the next flip.

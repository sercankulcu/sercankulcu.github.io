How to play

Identify 10 country flags. Pick the country each flag belongs to.

Rules

  • Each quiz contains 10 different countries.
  • Each question has four answer choices.
  • A correct answer gives 10 points.
  • An incorrect answer subtracts 3 points, with a minimum score of zero.

Controls

  • Tap an answer, or press 1–4 on your keyboard.
  • New Quiz starts over with 10 new countries.

Records

Best is your highest score. Your best score and the number of quizzes played are stored only on this device.

Language

The TR / EN button switches the interface and the country names between Turkish and English.

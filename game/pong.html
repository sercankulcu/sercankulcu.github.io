🏓 Pong

How to play

Classic Pong against the computer: keep the ball in play with your paddle and get it past the computer's.

How to play

  1. Pick a computer difficulty under the court: Easy, Medium or Hard.
  2. Press Start Game. You control the left paddle.
  3. Where the ball meets your paddle sets its angle; every hit makes it a little faster.
  4. First to 7 points wins.

Controls

  • Mouse: move it over the court.
  • Touch: drag or tap on the court.
  • Keyboard: W / S or ↑ / ↓.
  • P pauses and resumes; ↺ starts a new game.

Notes

  • The game pauses when you switch tabs or open this help.

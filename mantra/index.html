A guided mantra exercise for observing thoughts, slowing down and focusing through repetition.

How to use

  1. Read the short introduction and press Next to move through it, or press Skip to Mantra.
  2. A calm background sound starts during the introduction. Use Pause sound or Play sound under the panel to control it.
  3. The mantra of the day is shown in Sanskrit and in transliteration. Type the transliteration exactly as shown, including punctuation.
  4. The field turns red where your text stops matching. When it matches, press Complete or Enter.
  5. About this Mantra shows its source, meaning and context.
  6. The mantra itself stays in Sanskrit; TR / EN switches the interface language, and the choice is remembered for all tools.

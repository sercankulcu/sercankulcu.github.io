If I were building a gaming PC on **July 1, 2021**, this is the machine I would actually want. I am not simply choosing the most expensive part in every category. The priority is gaming performance, then reliability, cooling and enough headroom to keep the system useful for years.

### The build

- **CPU:** AMD Ryzen 9 5900X
- **GPU:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080 Ti 12 GB
- **Motherboard:** ASUS ROG Crosshair VIII Dark Hero (X570)
- **Memory:** 32 GB DDR4-3600 CL16
- **Storage:** Samsung 980 Pro 2 TB PCIe 4.0 NVMe
- **Power supply:** Corsair RM850x
- **Case:** Fractal Design Meshify 2
- **CPU cooling:** Arctic Liquid Freezer II 360
- **Gaming target:** 4K high settings with ray tracing/DLSS; 1440p very high refresh

### Why this CPU?

AMD launched the 5900X at $549 with 12 Zen 3 cores and marketed it directly around top gaming performance. It gives me excellent frame rates without sacrificing streaming, compiling or background workloads.

### Why this GPU?

The RTX 3080 Ti had launched only weeks earlier, on June 3, at $1,199 and NVIDIA called it its new gaming flagship. I choose it over the RTX 3090 because the 3090’s huge 24 GB memory pool is more useful to creators than to games.

### What I would pay

A complete build would be roughly **$2,500–3,000 at MSRP, often much more during the shortage** in the U.S. market of the period. This is an approximate enthusiast-system total, not a claim that every retailer sold every part at MSRP on July 1.

### The weak point

Availability. The semiconductor and crypto-era GPU shortage means MSRP is almost theoretical; actually finding the card may be harder than paying for it.

### My verdict

This is where I stop equating 'most expensive' with 'best gaming choice.' The 3080 Ti gives me flagship-class gaming without paying for 24 GB of memory I probably will not use.

The date matters. I am judging only hardware that was available by July 1, not products announced or released later in the year. That makes each build a snapshot of what I could realistically have assembled at that moment.

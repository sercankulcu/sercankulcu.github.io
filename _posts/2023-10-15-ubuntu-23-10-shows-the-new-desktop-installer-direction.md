Ubuntu 23.10 “Mantic Minotaur” was released in October 2023 with a newer Flutter-based desktop installer and GNOME 45. The installer represented Canonical's effort to share more installation technology across desktop variants while modernizing a component that had changed slowly for years.

The release also used newer Linux kernel and graphics components, which mattered for recently released hardware. Interim Ubuntu versions have only short support periods, so they act partly as staging grounds for technology that may later appear in long-term-support releases.

Ubuntu 23.10 briefly had to be withdrawn and rebuilt after malicious translations were discovered in part of the installer. That incident showed an unusual software-supply-chain risk: community localization content can also become an attack or abuse surface.

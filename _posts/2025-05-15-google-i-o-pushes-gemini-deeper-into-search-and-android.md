Google I/O 2025 showed the company moving Gemini from a standalone chatbot into Search, Android, developer tools, and creative applications. Gemini 2.5 models and agent-oriented features were central to the announcements.

Google demonstrated AI Mode in Search, Project Mariner-style browser agents, and Gemini Live capabilities that could use a phone's camera and screen as context. The company also introduced new media models such as Veo 3 and Imagen 4.

The technical shift was from models that only return text toward systems connected to tools, live sensory input, and product state. That increases usefulness but also expands the security boundary: an AI that can act on a user's behalf needs permission controls, reliable tool selection, and clear confirmation around consequential actions.

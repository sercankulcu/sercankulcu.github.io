November 10, 2023 marks eighty-five years since the death of Mustafa Kemal Atatürk. At 09:05, the moment of his death, daily life across Turkey pauses. I have always found this silence meaningful. It is rare for millions of people to stop at the same minute and remember one historical figure. Atatürk's importance cannot be separated from the foundation of the Republic of Turkey.

He led the Turkish National Movement, became the first president of the Republic, and supported major reforms in law, education, language, public institutions, and social life. Historical figures should be studied with seriousness rather than reduced to slogans. For me, remembrance becomes more meaningful when it includes reading, questioning, and understanding the conditions of the period. Atatürk repeatedly emphasized reason, science, education, and national sovereignty.

These ideas remain worth discussing because a republic is never a completed object. Each generation receives institutions and values from the previous one and decides what to do with them. Commemoration is not only looking backward. It can also be a reminder of responsibility in the present.

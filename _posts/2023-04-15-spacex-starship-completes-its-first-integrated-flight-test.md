SpaceX launched Starship and its Super Heavy booster together for the first integrated flight test in April 2023. The vehicle cleared the launch tower but several engines failed, control was lost, and the flight-termination system eventually destroyed the vehicle.

The test produced enormous amounts of telemetry from propulsion, guidance, structural, thermal, and flight-control systems. For an experimental vehicle, that data was part of the objective: engineers could compare actual behavior with simulations and update models before the next flight.

The event illustrated iterative aerospace development at extreme scale. Software and hardware were tested as one coupled system, and failure was treated as a source of engineering information rather than only as an endpoint.

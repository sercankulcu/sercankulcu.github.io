OpenAI's board removed CEO Sam Altman on November 17, 2023, triggering five days of extraordinary organizational turmoil. President Greg Brockman left, employees threatened to resign, Microsoft offered positions to Altman and others, and the board eventually reinstated Altman with a changed membership.

The crisis mattered technically because OpenAI operated some of the world's most widely used AI models and relied on a deep infrastructure partnership with Microsoft. Customers using its APIs suddenly had to consider governance risk alongside ordinary concerns such as uptime and pricing.

The episode demonstrated that critical AI infrastructure can depend on unusual corporate structures and small groups of decision-makers. Model weights and servers may be technical assets, but organizational stability can be just as important to developers building products on top of them.

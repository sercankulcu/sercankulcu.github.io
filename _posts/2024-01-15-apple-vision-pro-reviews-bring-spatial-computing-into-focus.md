Apple opened Vision Pro preorders in January 2024, and detailed hands-on testing began before the February retail launch. Reviewers focused on both the remarkable display and tracking quality and the physical limits of wearing a heavy computer on the face.

Vision Pro used two high-resolution micro-OLED displays and a large camera array. Eye tracking selected interface targets while hand tracking recognized small gestures, allowing interaction without controllers. The external battery kept some weight off the headset but introduced a cable.

The device made a key spatial-computing trade-off visible: better displays and sensors improve presence, but they also consume power, generate heat, and add weight. Comfort, field of view, latency, and social isolation can matter as much as raw processor performance.

Apple introduced the A13 Bionic processor with the iPhone 11 generation in September 2019. The chip used a 7 nm-class process and contained billions of transistors across CPU, GPU, image-processing, and machine-learning blocks.

Apple emphasized not only peak performance but fine-grained power management. Individual parts of the chip could be activated or slowed independently, important in a phone where thermal headroom and battery capacity are limited.

The Neural Engine and machine-learning accelerators supported photography features such as semantic image processing and later Deep Fusion. Smartphone photography was becoming a real-time computational pipeline: multiple camera frames were aligned, classified, merged, and sharpened before the user saw the final image.

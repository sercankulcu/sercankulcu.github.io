Apple announced the iPhone 17, iPhone Air, iPhone 17 Pro, and iPhone 17 Pro Max on September 9, 2025, with sales beginning September 19. The standard iPhone 17 used the A19 chip with a 6.3-inch ProMotion display and 48MP Fusion Main and Ultra Wide cameras. iPhone Air — replacing the Plus as the thin-and-light option — launched at 5.6mm thin, the slimmest iPhone ever, with the A19 Pro.

The iPhone 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max used the A19 Pro, built on TSMC's third-generation 3nm process (N3P). Apple cited up to 40 percent better sustained performance than the previous generation (with the new vapor chamber), a 6-core GPU with Neural Accelerators in each core, and a 16-core Neural Engine. The improved performance primarily targeted on-device Apple Intelligence features: Writing Tools, Image Playground, Genmoji generation, and expanded Siri context processing all ran on-device to avoid sending personal data to servers. iPhone 17 Pro added a 48MP ultrawide camera (up from 12MP), 4K 120fps video with Dolby Vision, and support for ProRes RAW and Apple Log 2.

The iPhone 17 lineup was the third iPhone generation built around Apple Intelligence, which Apple introduced in 2024. By 2025 the Neural Engine, with Neural Accelerators now in the GPU cores as well, had become a headline part of the chip story for flagship phones, alongside raw CPU and GPU throughput.

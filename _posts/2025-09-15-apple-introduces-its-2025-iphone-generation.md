Apple introduced the iPhone 17 generation in September 2025, continuing the company's shift toward processors and hardware designed around sustained on-device machine-learning workloads.

The new phones paired updated Apple silicon with camera, display, and wireless changes while Apple continued expanding system-level AI features. As with recent generations, the important architectural story was integration: image-signal processors, Neural Engine blocks, CPU, GPU, memory bandwidth, and power management all contributed to features users experienced as “camera” or “AI.”

Smartphone performance had become increasingly difficult to summarize with one CPU benchmark. The most important workloads often moved through several specialized accelerators before a result reached the screen.

Google I/O 2026 ran May 19–20 in Mountain View, with Sundar Pichai's keynote focused almost entirely on agentic AI applications. Google announced Gemini 3.5 Flash — the first model in a new family that pairs frontier intelligence with the ability to take actions, built for speed — and Gemini Omni, a model that starts with video output and accepts image, text, video, or audio references. Gemini Omni began rolling out to Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers.

Google added information agents to Search, along with Gemini Spark and Daily Brief in the Gemini app. Antigravity 2.0, Google's agent-first development platform, became a standalone desktop app in which multiple agents can run in parallel, with an early research preview of subagents that collaborate. Gmail gained an AI Inbox that prioritizes to-dos and drafts personalized replies.

Agentic AI introduced different engineering requirements from conversational AI: a model controlling browser sessions or writing files needed persistent session state across many tool calls, error recovery when a webpage returned unexpected content, and explicit checkpoints where the user could review and approve consequential actions before they were taken. Google said Gemini Spark is designed to check with the user before major actions.

Google announced the Pixel 9 family in August 2024, moving its usual flagship-phone launch earlier in the year. The lineup used Google's Tensor G4 processor and placed Gemini-based AI features at the center of the product.

The phones included more memory than earlier generations partly to support on-device AI workloads. Features such as Pixel Screenshots created searchable information from screenshots, while Gemini Live provided more conversational voice interaction.

The hardware illustrated how smartphone design was changing around AI. RAM capacity, NPUs, privacy boundaries, and model deployment were becoming product specifications alongside camera sensors and battery life. Google also controlled both Android and the Tensor platform, allowing software features to be designed around specific hardware capabilities.

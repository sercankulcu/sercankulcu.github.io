NASA released the first full-color scientific images from the James Webb Space Telescope in July 2022. The images were visually spectacular, but they were not simple camera snapshots.

Webb's instruments record calibrated detector data across infrared wavelengths. Processing pipelines correct detector effects, combine exposures, align observations, remove artifacts, and map infrared measurements into visible colors. Large scientific archives then make both processed products and underlying measurements available to researchers.

The telescope also depends on complex onboard and ground software for scheduling observations, pointing, instrument control, and communication from roughly 1.5 million kilometers away near the Sun–Earth L2 point. Webb demonstrated how modern astronomy is inseparable from data processing and software engineering.

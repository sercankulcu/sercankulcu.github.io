Twitter accepted Elon Musk's approximately $44 billion acquisition offer in April 2022. What followed was not a normal software product cycle but a prolonged legal and corporate struggle over whether the purchase would actually happen.

Musk had raised questions about spam and automated accounts, while Twitter argued that the signed merger agreement required him to complete the transaction. The dispute moved toward a Delaware court case before Musk ultimately proceeded with the purchase in October.

From a technology perspective, the episode placed unusual attention on Twitter's internal measurement systems: how the company estimated monetizable users, detected bots, and represented platform activity. Those metrics are not simple counts; they depend on classification systems, sampling methods, and definitions that can materially affect business claims.

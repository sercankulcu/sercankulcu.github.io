How to use

A minimal countdown timer with presets, keyboard shortcuts, fullscreen mode and browser notifications.

Steps

  1. Click or tap the time to set it: type the minutes or pick a preset, then press Set.
  2. Press Start. Use −1m and +1m to adjust while it runs, and Reset to go back to the full time.
  3. When the time is up you hear three beeps and the display pulses.

Notes

  • 🔕 / 🔔 turns browser notifications on or off; the browser asks for permission the first time.
  • The duration is kept in the address bar, so you can bookmark or share a timer.
  • TR / EN switches the interface language; the choice is remembered for all tools.

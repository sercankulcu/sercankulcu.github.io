    How to use

    Add, reorder and download your PDF files as a single document.

    1. Drop PDF files on the drop zone, or click it or Add Files to choose them.
    2. Set the order with the ↑ and ↓ buttons or by dragging a row; × removes a file.
    3. Press Merge to download all files as one PDF, in the order shown.
    4. After merging, “Continue with” passes the merged PDF straight to another tool.

    Notes

    • Files are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Unit Converter

How to use

Utility tool: fast browser-based conversions for everyday and technical units.

Steps

  1. Pick a category: length, mass, area, volume, speed, time, data or temperature.
  2. Type a value in From and choose the two units. The result updates as you type.
  3. Press ⇄ to swap the units, Copy Result to copy the conversion, and Reset to start over.

Notes

  • Non-temperature units are converted through the category's base unit; temperatures use their own formulas.
  • All conversions run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • TR / EN switches the interface language; the choice is remembered for all tools.

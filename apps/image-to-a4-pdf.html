How to use

Turn a long webpage screenshot, PNG, JPG or WebP into a multi-page A4 PDF. The image is split automatically while keeping its original proportions.

How to use

  1. Drop an image on the box or click it to choose a PNG, JPG, JPEG or WebP file.
  2. Pick the A4 orientation, a margin and the PDF image quality.
  3. Press Preview to see the page split, then Download PDF.
  4. Continue with passes the new PDF straight to another PDF tool.

Best for webpage screenshots

Use a full-page PNG screenshot. The app scales the image to the printable A4 width, then slices it vertically into consecutive pages.

Privacy

The image is processed in your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server.

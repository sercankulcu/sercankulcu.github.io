How to use

Image tool: resize, compress and convert images locally in your browser.

Steps

  1. Drop an image on the box or click it to choose one.
  2. Set the output Width and Height. With Keep aspect ratio on, changing one updates the other.
  3. Choose a Quality and an output Format (JPEG, WebP or PNG).
  4. Press Process Image, compare the original and output sizes, then press Download. Reset starts over.

Notes

  • Everything runs in your browser; the image is not uploaded.
  • Exported images do not preserve EXIF metadata.
  • You can paste an image with Ctrl+V, and pass the result on to another image tool with the Continue with buttons.

TR / EN switches the interface language; the choice is remembered for all tools.

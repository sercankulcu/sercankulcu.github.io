How to use

A brain test that measures how quickly you react to a visual signal.

How to use

  1. Click or tap the test area to start. It turns red.
  2. Wait for the area to turn green, then click as quickly as you can.
  3. Clicking while it is still red, or less than 100 ms after it turns green, counts as too soon and the attempt does not count.
  4. Five valid attempts are averaged. Last, average and best times appear under the area.

Controls

  • Mouse or touch: click or tap the area.
  • Keyboard: focus the area and press Enter or Space.
  • TR / EN switches the interface language; the choice is remembered for all tools.

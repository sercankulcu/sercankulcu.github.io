How to use

Draw a signature or photograph one on white paper, then export it as a transparent PNG.

Draw a signature

  1. Sign in the white box with your mouse, finger or stylus.
  2. Pick the ink color; use Clear to start again.
  3. Press Download Transparent PNG.

Use a photo

  1. Choose or drop a photo of your signature (on a phone this can open the camera).
  2. Adjust the white removal threshold and the signature color.
  3. Press Process Photo, then Download Transparent PNG.

Tip

For photographed signatures, use dark ink on clean white paper and even lighting.

Privacy

Everything happens in your browser; your signature is not uploaded to a server.

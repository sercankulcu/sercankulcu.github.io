How to use

Draw your signature with a finger or mouse, choose a page, tap the preview to place it, then download the signed PDF.

How to use

  1. Drop a PDF on the drop zone, or click it to choose a file.
  2. Enter the page that should carry the signature.
  3. Draw your signature in the signature pad. Clear Signature starts it over.
  4. Tap the PDF preview where the signature should be centered.
  5. Adjust the width and ink color, then press Download Signed PDF.
  6. Continue with passes the signed PDF straight to another tool.

Keyboard

  • Tab to the drop zone and press Enter or Space to choose a file.
  • Focus the preview and use the arrow keys to move the signature.

Notes

  • Everything is processed in your browser. The PDF is never uploaded.
  • This adds a visual signature; it is not a cryptographic digital signature.

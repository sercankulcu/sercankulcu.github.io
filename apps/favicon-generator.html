How to use

Web asset tool: turn any image into a clean favicon set. Crop it to a square, adjust padding and background, then download ready-to-use PNG files.

Steps

  1. Drop an image on the box or click it to choose one (PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP). Square images work best.
  2. Pick a fit mode: Contain keeps the whole image, Cover fills the square and crops the edges.
  3. Set the padding, corner radius and background colour, or tick Transparent background.
  4. Check the small previews, then download.
  5. You can also paste an image with Ctrl+V, or arrive here with an image from another tool.

Downloads

  • Download All PNGs gives a ZIP with 16, 32 and 48 px favicons, apple-touch-icon.png (180 px), the 192 and 512 px Android icons, a site.webmanifest and favicon-links.txt with the HTML tags to paste into your page.
  • Download favicon-32x32.png saves only the most common size.

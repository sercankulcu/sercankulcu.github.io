How to use

Convert JPEG, PNG, and WebP images locally in your browser — no server, no upload.

Steps

  1. Drop images on the box or click it to browse. You can add several at once (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP).
  2. Choose the Output format and, for JPEG or WebP, a Quality. PNG is lossless, so quality is not used.
  3. Press Convert all. Each card then shows the new size and gets its own Download button.
  4. Press Download ZIP to save every converted image at once, or Clear to start over.

Notes

  • Images are converted in your browser and are never uploaded.
  • You can paste images with Ctrl+V, and pass the converted files on to another image tool with the Continue with buttons.

How to use

A mobile camera tool: take or choose a document photo, rotate it, enhance contrast, switch to grayscale or black & white, then save as image or A4 PDF.

How to use

  1. Tap Take / Choose Photo (on a phone this opens the camera), or drop an image on the box.
  2. Pick a filter and adjust brightness and contrast; use Rotate 90° if needed.
  3. Save with Download JPG or Download A4 PDF.
  4. After the PDF download, Continue with passes the PDF straight to another PDF tool.

Tip

For best results, photograph the page straight-on with good lighting.

Privacy

The photo is processed in your browser; it is not uploaded to a server.

TR / EN switches the interface language; the choice is remembered for all tools.

Dominant colors

How to use

Extract dominant colors from any image. Click a swatch to copy its value.

Steps

  1. Drop an image on the box or click it to browse (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP).
  2. Choose the Number of colors; the palette updates at once. Re-extract runs the analysis again.
  3. Pick a Copy format (HEX, RGB or HSL), then click a swatch or its Copy button.
  4. Copy CSS vars copies the whole palette as CSS custom properties.

Notes

  • The image is analysed in your browser and is not uploaded.

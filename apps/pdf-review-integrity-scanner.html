How to use

Detect suspicious or hidden instructions that may be intended to influence a peer reviewer, automated reviewer, or AI-assisted review process.

How to use

  1. Drop a submitted PDF on the box, or click it to browse your computer.
  2. Wait while every page is read and scanned.
  3. Check the overall risk, the counters and each finding with its page and reasons.
  4. Use Copy report to paste the result elsewhere, or Scan another PDF.

What it looks for

Designed to surface review-influencing instructions, prompt-injection patterns, and suspiciously hidden text (for example very small or invisible text).

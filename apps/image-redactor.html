How to use

Privacy tool: hide sensitive information in screenshots or photos with black boxes, white boxes or pixelation.

Steps

  1. Click, tap or drop a screenshot or photo on Choose Image.
  2. Pick a Redaction mode: black box, white box or pixelate.
  3. Drag directly on the image (mouse, pen or finger) over each area you want to hide.
  4. Press Download PNG. Undo removes the last box and Reset removes them all.

Keyboard

  • Ctrl+Z undoes the last box.

Notes

  • Redactions are baked into the downloaded image.
  • For privacy, use opaque black or white redaction rather than blur when you need irreversible hiding.
  • The image is edited in your browser and is not uploaded.

Privacy Tool

Image Redactor

Hide sensitive information in screenshots or photos. Drag over areas to apply black boxes, white boxes or pixelation.

Choose Image

Drag directly on the image. Redactions are baked into the downloaded image.

Choose an image to begin.

For privacy, use opaque black or white redaction rather than blur when you need irreversible hiding.

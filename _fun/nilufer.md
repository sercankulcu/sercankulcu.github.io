Some songs are about love while it is still alive. *Nilüfer* begins after everything is already over.

It carries the strange pain of loving someone who is no longer part of your life. The relationship may have ended, the distance may have grown, and both people may have continued in different directions. Yet one person still remains somewhere inside the other. This is one of the hardest things about separation. A relationship can end much earlier than the feelings connected to it.

When two people are together, love is easy to understand. You can speak, meet, argue, forgive, make plans, change things. But when the person is gone, the feeling may continue with nowhere to go. You cannot call whenever you want. You cannot repair every mistake. Sometimes you cannot even say goodbye properly.

Then love changes its form.

It becomes memory. It becomes silence. It becomes a song you avoid for months and then suddenly listen to one evening.

After a long separation, I may no longer miss the real person. I may miss the person I remember.

This difference is important. People change. I change too. Someone I loved ten years ago may now be almost a stranger. Still, the version of that person inside my memory remains young, familiar, and close. Memory protects people from time. It keeps one smile, one conversation, one evening. It removes ordinary details and leaves a cleaner version of the past. That is why old love can sometimes become stronger in memory than it ever was in real life. I am not only missing a person. I am missing a time.

Two people can live through the same relationship and remember it in completely different ways. One remembers the beautiful beginning. The other remembers the difficult ending. One remembers the promises. The other remembers the disappointments. Neither memory has to be false. Human memory is selective. We keep the parts that explain our own feelings. Years later, we may even change the story without realizing it. Pain becomes softer. The person who once caused sleepless nights becomes part of an old chapter.

Understanding often comes too late. When I was inside a relationship, I sometimes focused on being right, protecting myself, winning an argument. Only after losing someone did I sometimes understand what was really important. Life gives us wisdom slowly, but does not always give us the chance to use it on the same people.

For me, *Nilüfer* is about the place where love, memory, and regret meet.

There is no great solution in this kind of story. Some people do not return. Some conversations never happen. Some mistakes cannot be corrected. I have to learn to live with that. Perhaps growing older means understanding that not every important story in my life will have a perfect ending. Some people stay with us only for a few years, but their influence lasts much longer.

Eventually, the pain becomes quieter. I stop waiting. I stop imagining another ending. I accept that the person belongs to another part of my life.

But then, one evening, a familiar song begins.

For a few minutes, everything returns. Not because I want the past back. Not because I have failed to move on. Simply because once, somewhere in my life, I loved someone deeply.

And the heart does not completely delete what was once important.

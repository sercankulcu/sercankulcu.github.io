Some songs are about love while it is still alive. Others begin after everything is already over.

*Nilüfer* feels like the second kind.

It carries the strange pain of loving someone who is no longer part of your life. The relationship may have ended, the distance may have grown, and both people may have continued in different directions. Yet one person still remains somewhere inside the other.

This is one of the hardest things about separation. A relationship can end much earlier than the feelings connected to it.

## When Love Has Nowhere to Go

Love is easy to understand when two people are together. You can speak, meet, argue, forgive, make plans, and change things.

But what happens when the person is gone?

The feeling may continue, but it has nowhere to go.

You cannot call whenever you want. You cannot explain the same things again. You cannot repair every mistake. Sometimes you cannot even say goodbye properly.

Then love changes its form.

It becomes memory.

It becomes silence.

It becomes a song you avoid for months and then suddenly listen to one evening.

## The Person and the Memory

After a long separation, we may no longer miss the real person. We may miss the person we remember.

This difference is important.

People change. We change too. Someone we loved ten years ago may now be almost a stranger. Still, the version of that person inside our memory remains young, familiar, and close.

Memory protects people from time.

It keeps one smile, one conversation, one evening, and one feeling. It removes many ordinary details and leaves us with a cleaner version of the past.

That is why old love can sometimes become stronger in memory than it ever was in real life.

We are not only missing a person.

We are missing a time.

## Pride and Silence

Many relationships do not end because love disappears completely.

Sometimes they end because two people become tired.

Sometimes there is pride.

Sometimes the same argument happens too many times.

Sometimes both people wait for the other one to speak first.

Then silence becomes longer.

A day becomes a week. A week becomes a month. Eventually, silence becomes normal.

This is one of the saddest forms of distance because both people may still have something to say.

But there comes a point when even the right words arrive too late.

## We Remember Differently

Two people can live through the same relationship and remember it in completely different ways.

One remembers the beautiful beginning.

The other remembers the difficult ending.

One remembers the promises.

The other remembers the disappointments.

Neither memory has to be false.

Human memory is selective. We keep the parts that explain our own feelings.

Years later, we may even change the story without realizing it. Pain becomes softer. Anger loses its strength. The person who once caused us sleepless nights becomes part of an old chapter.

Time does not always make us forget.

Sometimes it simply changes the meaning of what we remember.

## The Love That Arrives Too Late

There is another kind of sadness in old relationships.

Understanding often comes too late.

When we are inside a relationship, we may focus on being right, protecting ourselves, or winning an argument. Only after losing someone do we sometimes understand what was really important.

Then many questions appear.

Why did I not listen more?

Why did I make that small problem so large?

Why did I not say what I really felt?

These questions are easy to ask after everything is finished.

Life gives us wisdom slowly, but unfortunately it does not always give us the chance to use that wisdom on the same people.

## Nilüfer

For me, *Nilüfer* is about the place where love, memory, and regret meet.

It is not the hopeful beginning of love. It is what remains after the noise is gone.

There is no great solution in this kind of story. Some people do not return. Some conversations never happen. Some mistakes cannot be corrected.

We have to learn to live with that.

Perhaps growing older means understanding that not every important story in our life will have a perfect ending.

Some people stay with us only for a few years, but their influence lasts much longer.

They change the music we listen to, the places we remember, and sometimes even the person we become.

Eventually, the pain becomes quieter.

You stop waiting.

You stop imagining another ending.

You accept that the person belongs to another part of your life.

But then, one evening, a familiar song begins.

For a few minutes, everything returns.

Not because you want the past back.

Not because you have failed to move on.

Simply because once, somewhere in your life, you loved someone deeply.

And the heart does not completely delete what was once important.

Being loved is not always the same as being seen.

*Sevmemeliyiz* understands this very well. There is something more painful than somebody leaving you. It is having them close enough to know you, yet somehow feeling invisible beside them.

You can talk for hours and still feel unheard. You can tell somebody exactly what hurts you, and somehow they understand every word except the important part. After a while, you begin to wonder whether you are asking for too much.

Maybe I expect too much.

Maybe this is simply what love is.

I think many people stay too long because of these questions.

Understanding requires more than knowledge. Someone can know your history, your habits, your favorite things, and still miss the person behind all of it. Knowledge is a catalogue. Understanding requires attention — and more than that, curiosity. The willingness to notice when the person you love is slowly becoming quieter.

That last part matters.

People rarely disappear from a relationship in one day. They become tired first. They explain less. They stop correcting misunderstandings because speaking about them no longer seems useful. From outside, everything may still look normal.

Inside, something is already ending.

I have learned that love does not become healthier simply because it is strong. A feeling can be deep and still be wrong for your life. You can love someone sincerely and still become less yourself beside them. The title *Sevmemeliyiz* feels sad rather than cold to me because of this — it does not sound like someone who has decided love is useless. It sounds like someone who has loved enough to understand the cost.

Maybe there are people we should not love.

Not because they are terrible people.

Not because the feeling is unreal.

But because loving them slowly teaches us to ignore ourselves.

And no love should require that.

Being understood is one of the most intimate things another person can give us. Not agreement with everything we say, not perfect knowledge of every thought, but the feeling that when we speak, somebody is actually there. They see us. They make the effort to know the person behind the words.

Without that, even love can become lonely.

Perhaps that is the strangest pain of all.

To have someone beside you and still miss being found.

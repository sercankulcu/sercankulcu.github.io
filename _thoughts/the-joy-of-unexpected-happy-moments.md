Some of my favorite moments are the ones I did not plan. A small cafe discovered while walking somewhere else. Meeting an old friend by accident. Finding a book I had forgotten. Hearing a song at exactly the right time.

These moments are often called serendipity. They are happy accidents. I like them because they arrive without the pressure of expectation. When we plan something carefully, the real experience has to compete with the picture in our mind. An unexpected moment has no picture to compete with. It only happens.

Modern life gives us many tools for planning. Maps tell us where to go. Reviews tell us where to eat. Search engines tell us what to read. Calendars protect our time.

These tools are useful. But perfect planning can remove some opportunities for surprise. Sometimes it is nice not to optimize everything. Take the slower street. Enter the unfamiliar shop.

Say yes to a small invitation. Leave a little empty space in the day. Serendipity cannot be scheduled. That is exactly why it feels different. Maybe happiness is not always something we build directly. Sometimes it is something we notice when life briefly moves away from the plan.

---
title: 'Do Not Judge People Who Leave Without Saying Goodbye'
date: 2023-03-13
tags:
  - life
  - relationships
  - self-improvement
---

When someone leaves without saying goodbye, the silence can hurt more than the departure. We naturally want an explanation. Was I the problem? Were they angry? Did the relationship mean less to them than it meant to me?

The mind fills empty space quickly. Usually it fills it with the explanation that hurts most. But people leave quietly for many reasons. Fear. Shame.

Exhaustion. A situation they do not know how to explain. Sometimes they believe disappearing will create less pain than a difficult conversation. Sometimes they are simply avoiding responsibility. We cannot know from silence alone.

This does not mean ghosting or disappearing is always acceptable. People deserve honesty when honesty is possible. But judging another person's whole character from one unexplained exit may be too easy. There can be a difference between understanding a behavior and approving it. I can accept that someone had reasons I do not know and still believe the way they left hurt me.

Both things can be true. Eventually, not every goodbye arrives in words. Sometimes closure has to be created by the person who remains. That is unfair. But waiting forever for an explanation can become another kind of absence.
